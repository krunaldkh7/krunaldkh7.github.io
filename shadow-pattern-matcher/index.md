A single spotlight. A tangled sculpture of gold wire. Somewhere in its shadow, a picture is hiding.

Turn the wire, twist it, and watch its shadow on the wall until it matches the outline.
163 shapes across five galleries, your own drawn levels, and a global leaderboard.
